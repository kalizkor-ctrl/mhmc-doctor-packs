How the prices were set

A package should be a comfortable spend for a well-off family, not a stretch. We used 3% of monthly household income as comfortable and 5% as the ceiling. In Lubao, a top-20% household earns about ₱45,300 a month and a top-10% household about ₱60,300. We assume one income pays for the package: the main earner brings about three quarters of the family figure, so the budget is taken from that. The Essential and Family levels aim at ₱1,019 to ₱1,699 a month, and Premium at ₱1,357 to ₱2,261 a month.

How each level is sized: every package covers a family of up to 5 people. Contents assume the whole family uses most of the treatments: 2 doctor visits per person a year, a massage every two months for everyone (monthly on the massage-led sets), one flu shot per person where the set includes vaccination, an acupuncture course for each adult where the set is about pain, and one TCM treatment session per person on Family, two on Premium. The saving is 20% on Essential, 30% on Family and 40% on Premium, so the ladder pulls upward.

Every level is priced from what the same visits cost bought one by one, using our list prices: consultation ₱600, Swedish massage ₱500, acupuncture ₱1,700, flu shot ₱900, Massage + Ventosa or Gua Sha ₱850. The saving is taken off consultations and treatments, never off vaccines.

What these households spend on health

Filipino families put about 3.1% of what they spend into health (PSA family spending survey), and well-off families spend roughly four fifths of their income. The table turns that into pesos per household. It is all health spending, so clinics, medicines, labs and pharmacies together; our share of it is what a package competes for.

Rules that apply to every package

  • Sessions are shared among the named family members; a consultation counts per person; an acupuncture course stays with the person the doctor assessed.
  • Vaccines are never discounted; the saving comes from consultations and treatments.
  • Ventosa, Gua Sha and Moxibustion are add-ons quoted separately after the doctor checks suitability.
  • Sell the smallest level that matches how often the family expects to come, and book the first visits at purchase.
  • If a treatment course becomes unsuitable, unused sessions are refunded at their package value.
  • Six and twelve month packages can be paid in two or four instalments, with sessions released as instalments are paid.

What we assumed

Show the assumptions and sources
  • Evidence boundary: verified regional and provincial income figures are means, not medians or percentile thresholds. All median, top-20% and top-10% fields are analyst estimates using 2023 income anchors; they are not verified September 2026 household incomes.
  • Reproducible distribution assumption: monthly income is lognormal with sigma=0.65, selected for planning rather than fitted to local microdata. For monthly mean M, percentile p = M × exp(-0.65²/2 + 0.65 × z_p), with z50=0, z80=0.841621 and z90=1.281552; round to PHP 100. Lubao's assumed mean is 0.90 × Pampanga's mean. A 0.80–1.00 Lubao multiplier gives approximately PHP 40,200–50,300 for its top-20% threshold and PHP 53,600–67,000 for its top-10% threshold; this is scenario sensitivity, not a confidence interval.
  • FIES measures families; the requested household-income fields use family income as a proxy. Census household sizes are all-household averages, not measured sizes of affluent families. Packages therefore name actual beneficiaries instead of assuming every affluent household has five members.
  • Do not interpret decile averages or per-capita-ranked deciles as household-income entry thresholds. Official percentile validation requires appropriately weighted family-level income microdata. PSA 2023 FIES data documentation
  • PIDS's 2021-price middle-income range of PHP 24,060–144,360 monthly for five members is a poverty-line-based classification, not a top-20% or top-10% cutoff. It is contextual evidence only. PIDS Development Research News, 2024
  • OFW-remittance share is defined here as overseas receipts divided by total family income. Where local observations are missing, 8.5% may be used only as an explicitly assumed regional proxy; it does not estimate the percentage of households receiving remittances. The 2021 FIES foreign-cash category is broader than OFW-only transfers. Among Philippine OFW households, 56.9% reported allocating remittances to medical expenses in Q4 2023; that is a household-use incidence, not the percentage of remittance money spent on health. BSP Consumer Expectations Survey, Q4 2023, Table 12
  • The verified local health benchmark is regional and historical: approximately PHP 8,377 per family annually in 2018. Applying the same 3.1% expenditure share to Central Luzon's 2023 mean expenditure of PHP 298,700 gives an illustrative PHP 9,260 annually or PHP 772 monthly, not an observed 2023 health-spend statistic. It includes health spending beyond MHMC; affluent wellness customers are a selected segment. PSA 2023 regional expenditure
  • Pricing basis, September 2026: the user supplied only an approximate PHP 399–599 massage range and the existence of Acupuncture Package A. For this proposal, M means one standard Swedish massage at an assumed PHP 499; C means one individual GP consultation at an assumed PHP 500; A means one acupuncture session at an assumed PHP 800; V means one seasonal influenza dose including vaccine, administration and routine eligibility screening at an assumed PHP 1,000. Duration, vaccine product and all unit fees require MHMC confirmation. These are scenario retail values, not verified current tariffs.
  • RetailValuePHP = 499M + 500C + 800A + 1000V. PricePHP is the proposed selling price; discountPct = 100 × (retailValuePHP − pricePHP) / retailValuePHP, rounded to two decimals. Full vaccine retail value remains undiscounted internally; the total package saving is assigned to consultations and treatments. Routine vaccination screening is never charged again as a GP consultation.
  • Existing Acupuncture Package A contains three sessions and one consultation, but its price was not supplied. Its assumed itemized equivalent is PHP 2,900. Before advertising savings, compare each proposal with the cheapest existing combination customers can already buy, including Package A; reprice if the claimed saving would otherwise be overstated.
  • Common terms: counts are pooled among named family members unless a treatment course or vaccine allocation specifies otherwise; a consultation counts per person. Courses stay with the assessed patient. Paid add-ons are excluded from retailValuePHP and carry no automatic package discount. No monetary value is assigned to reminders, scheduling or records review.
  • Margin rule, analyst proposal September 2026: model 100% redemption and include clinician time, therapist pay, consumables, vaccine procurement and wastage, payment fees and incremental administration. Require at least 35% contribution margin after these direct costs; therefore direct cost must be no more than 65% of pricePHP. This is a proposed commercial hurdle, not verified MHMC profitability.
  • Utilization rule, analyst proposal September 2026: sell the smallest level matching the family's documented expected use, book the first appointments at purchase, and send reminders. Do not rely on expired credits to make margins work. If a course becomes clinically inappropriate, refund unused units at their allocated net package price; make no automatic annual treatment commitment.
  • Mother-and-child levels target families beyond the newborn period and are not complete infant-immunization, antenatal or pediatric specialist packages. Routine public-program vaccines should be coordinated with the family's health center. Additional childhood, HPV, pneumococcal or other vaccines require a record-specific quotation.
  • Ventosa, Gua Sha and Moxibustion have no supplied MHMC prices. Offer them only as separately quoted, physician-approved add-ons or substitutions after checking time, cost and suitability; do not invent a free bonus value.
  • The research draft priced with guessed tariffs (massage ₱499, consultation ₱500, acupuncture ₱800, flu ₱1,000). Every price above was recomputed with our real list prices, keeping the same percentage saving per level.
