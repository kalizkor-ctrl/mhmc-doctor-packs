What the page says

A 30-minute facial session combining gentle cleansing, hand massage and light-pressure gua sha.

  • This is a facial wellness session for adults with healthy, intact skin. The therapist checks the skin before beginning and defers the session when the skin is inflamed, wounded, sunburned or in a flare, and after recent injections, laser treatment or peels.
  • Research on facial gua sha is limited. A temporary change in puffiness may be noticed, but the session is not presented as detoxification, lifting, facial fat removal or a lasting change.
  • The massage and gua sha use light pressure. No marks are aimed for, and the therapist stops if you report pain or burning, or if bruising begins.

The 30 minutes, step by step

  • About 3 minutes to screen the skin and settle in, followed by about 4 minutes of gentle cleansing.
  • About 2 minutes to apply a fragrance-free slip product.
  • About 10 minutes of hand massage using cheek sweeps and work around the jaw, temples and forehead.
  • About 7 minutes of facial gua sha with a smooth tool held nearly flat and used with light pressure across the jaw, cheeks and forehead. The neck is not worked.
  • About 4 minutes to hydrate and finish with moisturiser and daytime SPF.

Who it is not for

Adults with healthy, intact skin only. The therapist defers when skin is inflamed, wounded, sunburned or in a flare (acne, rosacea, eczema), and after recent injections, laser or peels. No neck work. No marks aimed for.
